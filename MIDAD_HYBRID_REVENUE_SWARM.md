# MIDAD Hybrid Revenue Swarm

## Purpose

MIDAD converts intelligence into verified revenue through a reusable execution loop:

`DISCOVER → LEGAL EDGE → SCORE → CLAIM/PROPOSE → BUILD → QA → SUBMIT → ACCEPTANCE → PAYMENT → RECONCILE → LEARN`

## Revenue priority

Rank opportunities by revenue velocity rather than nominal reward:

`expected_net_value × acceptance_probability × payment_confidence ÷ estimated_minutes`

Use first-mover freshness, competition pressure, claimability, and automation readiness as additional signals.

## Global market mesh

The runtime supports regional lanes for:

- Arab Gulf
- Arab Levant & Iraq
- Arab North Africa
- South Asia
- East & Southeast Asia
- North America
- Latin America
- Europe & UK
- Global remote / crypto-native

Each lane carries language, currency, payment-network, platform-preference, and legal-review metadata.

## Mobile Factory monetization

The existing MIDAD Mobile Factory is a revenue capability, not only an internal tool. Product outputs can be packaged as:

- one-time mobile apps
- subscriptions
- white-label apps
- B2B licenses
- custom builds
- lead-generation products

The Mobile shell remains shared; products specialize the capability graph and manifest rather than cloning backend services.

## Legal Edge

Legal Edge maximizes the authorized action space while hard-stopping:

- unauthorized access
- credential theft
- authentication or platform bypass
- impersonation
- fraud
- data exfiltration
- malware/persistence
- account takeover

High-impact, irreversible, identity, KYC, signing, withdrawal, and regulated actions remain gated.

## Payment truth

Only independently verified settlement is counted as realized revenue. Applications, promises, prize pools, invoices, or pending states are not cash.

## Failure recovery

Rejected, expired, stale, blocked, or failed opportunities must be converted into a recovery case and evaluated for resubmission, repurposing, direct sale, or productization.

## Automation boundary

n8n is optional orchestration. Supabase remains the durable core. Browser agents and external tools are adapters around policy-controlled actions, never privileged bypass paths.

## Revenue Mesh v2 — Critical Execution Mode

**Primary metric:** verified receipt.

**Execution pattern:**
`DISCOVER (parallel) → VERIFY → RANK → PROPOSE/CLAIM → BUILD → QA → DELIVER → ACCEPT → VERIFY PAYMENT → RECONCILE → LEARN`

**Anti-stagnation rule:** a blocked lane must not stall the swarm. MIDAD must open the next qualified lane while retaining the blocked lane for recovery.

**Proposal quality rule:** external proposals must be task-specific and materially differentiated. Repeated/generic submissions are treated as a technical defect.

**Direct-sale rule:** productized services may be sold through MIDAD Public Checkout with the existing USDC/Solana receipt-verification pipeline.

**Persistence:** this mesh is a project operating policy and should be restored when MIDAD resumes after interruptions.
