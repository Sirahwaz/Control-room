## Important discovery: listing-feed freshness is unreliable

Several cards on Superteam category/region feeds showed apparent future countdowns, while their detail pages already displayed past winner-announcement dates and winners. Examples rechecked in this pass:

- [Try out new docs to build a trusted agent with T3N](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/t3n-agent-build-challenge): winners displayed; announcement date 23 Sep 2026.
- [Build and Demo a Mermail Agent Skill](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/build-and-demo-a-mermail-agent-skill): winners displayed; announcement date 18 Oct 2026.
- [Create an App on Cookie Chain](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/create-an-app-on-cookie-chain-app): winners displayed; announcement date 28 Sep 2026.
- [Steve Agent Arena](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/steve-agent-arena-launch-your-agent-and-win-500-usdc/): winners displayed; announcement date 4 Oct 2026.
- [$1,000 USDC Manic Bug Bounty](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/dollar1000-usdc-manic-bug-bounty): winners displayed; announcement date 16 Sep 2026.

Accordingly, a feed card or “days remaining” label must never be sufficient to mark an opportunity active. The detail page, explicit entry cutoff, and winner state must be checked directly.
